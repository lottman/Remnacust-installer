Plan around one anchor

Choose one activity you care about, then build a generous route around it. Avoid turning every stop into a timed appointment.

Keep a return option

Save the address of your accommodation and your transport details offline. Check current access, public transport and local advice rather than relying on an old screenshot.

Spend with attention

Use local businesses respectfully, keep public spaces clean and make room for the people who use the neighbourhood every day.
