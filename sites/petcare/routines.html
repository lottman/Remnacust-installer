Care that fits into a day.

These are general household reminders, not veterinary advice. Follow your veterinarian’s guidance for your individual animal.

Food and water

Use an appropriate food and a measured amount agreed with your veterinarian or the product guidance. Changes in appetite or drinking deserve attention; a checklist cannot explain their cause.

Movement and rest

Choose play and exercise suited to the animal’s age, health and comfort. Give cats places to hide and climb safely. Let dogs explore at a comfortable pace and rest afterward.

A shared care note

When several people help, keep a clear note of meals, walks and any instructions. Never rely on memory for prescribed medication; follow the veterinarian’s directions exactly.

Notice changes early

A new behaviour, altered movement or a change in eating can be useful information for a veterinarian. Seek professional help for health concerns rather than treating an online routine as a diagnosis.
