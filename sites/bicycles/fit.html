Find a frame-size starting point.

A rough seat-tube estimate from inseam length. Modern geometries vary widely; stack, reach and a real test ride matter more than the label.

Measure with care.

Stand barefoot with your back against a wall. Use a level book to measure from the floor to the crotch. Repeat the measurement before relying on it.

This is a traditional sizing rule, not a fit prescription. Compact frames, suspension and manufacturer sizing can differ substantially. Check standover clearance and comfort.
