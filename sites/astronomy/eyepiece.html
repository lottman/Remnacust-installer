What does an eyepiece change?

Compare magnification and an approximate true field of view. Actual usable fields depend on the eyepiece and telescope design.

Start with the wider view.

Magnification is telescope focal length divided by eyepiece focal length. A simple field estimate divides the apparent eyepiece field by that magnification.

The estimate ignores field stops and distortion. It is a way to compare setups, not a guarantee of optical quality or useful magnification.
