A session that stays manageable.

A practical home-recording guide. There are no studio bookings or uploaded recordings on this site.

Prepare the room

Turn off avoidable noise sources and listen from the recording position. Soft furnishings can reduce reflections, but they are not a substitute for understanding the room. Move the microphone before reaching for processing.

Keep a clean signal

Set levels with the loudest expected passage and leave headroom. Avoid clipping at the input; turning down a clipped recording later cannot restore the missing waveform.

Name as you go

Name tracks, takes and versions so that tomorrow’s session is understandable. Keep an untouched copy of the original recording and a separate project backup.

Finish one idea

Choose a limited set of sounds and complete a short arrangement. Take breaks at a comfortable listening level. A finished sketch teaches more than a large folder of unnamed loops.
