About Clay & form.

A study of useful ceramic forms: the small bowl, the generous cup and the vase that leaves room for a branch. Explore proportion before decoration.

A useful place to begin

Clay & form studies the proportions and practical details of useful vessels. Its illustrated forms are original reference studies for observing and sketching.

Using this notebook

The shrinkage calculator uses your own measured percentage. It is not a firing schedule or a food-safety assessment. Use tested material information and workshop guidance.

Your privacy

Tools run in this browser. Entries are not uploaded, and the pages load no remote fonts, analytics or advertising. Saved choices and checklist states use this browser’s local storage, scoped to this notebook. Clear site data in your browser to remove them. Downloads are files created in your browser, not files sent to a service. The hosting server may keep ordinary access logs according to its owner’s settings. No accounts, orders or bookings are accepted here.
