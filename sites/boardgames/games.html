Small games you can make.

Original microgame ideas using paper, a pencil and ordinary dice. Agree on the rules before starting; no commercial games are represented.

Search by material or choose a style of play.

Dice

Race to thirty

Take turns rolling one six-sided die. Add it to your score or roll again. A one loses the points from this turn. Bank before ending a turn. First to 30 wins.

2–4

Cooperative

The shared total

As a group, roll six dice one at a time and choose plus or minus for each result. Aim to finish as close to 12 as possible. Everyone discusses each choice.

2

Paper

Three by three

Draw a 3×3 grid. Alternate writing unused numbers 1–9 in empty cells. Player A scores the top and bottom rows; B scores the left and right columns. Highest total wins.

2–6

Paper

Word ladder

Choose a four-letter starting word. Each player changes one letter to form a different word. No repeats. If a player cannot move, start a new ladder and give the previous player a point.

2–6

Cooperative

Quiet collection

Each person writes three ordinary objects on slips. Draw one and describe it without its name. The group gets one point if someone guesses within one minute.

2–6

Dice

Closest pair

Everyone rolls two dice three times, keeping their lowest difference. Ties use the higher total of that pair. After five rounds, the most round wins takes the game.
