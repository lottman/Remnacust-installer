Make room for a good fit.

Measure both feet while standing, in the socks you plan to wear. This is an approximate starting point, never a replacement for the maker’s chart.

Try the whole shoe.

Measure late in the day, with your heel against a straight wall. Use the longer foot. Stand, walk and check that your heel stays secure without pinching the toes.

EU sizing varies by maker and last. Compare the suggested inner length with an actual size chart and use any available return policy.
