Three ways to read a room.

Concept studies for learning and sketching, not built projects or professional construction advice.

The useful threshold

A place to put down a bag and take off shoes changes how an entrance works. Keep this activity out of the main circulation path.

Arrival

Light across a wall

A side-lit surface reveals texture and gives a room depth. Sketch how direct sun moves through the space before committing to a desk position.

Daylight

The quiet edge

Seating feels different when its back is protected and its view is open. Try an existing chair in several positions before buying anything new.

Rest

Draw what is already there

Measure the room, openings, radiators and fixed services. Record dimensions in one unit and keep a separate list of measurements you have not confirmed.

Test with a paper plan

Cut furniture footprints from paper at the same scale as your room. Move them around and preserve a clear walking route. A beautiful arrangement is not useful if a door cannot open.

Know when to ask for help

Do not treat a sketch as permission to change structural walls, ventilation, wiring or fire escape routes. Consult a qualified local professional for any work affecting building safety.
