Keep the pattern small.

Eight steps make one bar of eighth notes in common time. Try a sound on steps 1, 3, 5 and 7, then move just one of them.

The pattern is saved in this browser. Playback stops when you leave the page or hide the tab. This simple workbench is not a precision recording clock.
