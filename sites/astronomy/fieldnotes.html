A notebook for after dusk.

Use a current sky chart for your date, location and hemisphere. This site does not fetch weather, position or ephemeris data.

Find the horizon

Choose a safe, permitted observing spot with a clear view and an easy way home. Note trees, buildings and artificial light sources before dark.

Start wide

Learn a few bright stars and large patterns with the unaided eye. Binoculars can be a useful next step, but a stable posture and a simple chart matter more than magnification.

Keep a short log

Record the date, approximate time, conditions and what you actually noticed. A sketch with a few labelled points is often more useful than a long list of targets.

Protect your eyes and equipment

Never point binoculars or a telescope at the Sun without a properly designed and correctly fitted solar observing system and expert guidance. Do not look through an optical finder at the Sun.
