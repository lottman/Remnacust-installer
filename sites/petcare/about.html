About Good company.

An everyday care notebook for people living with cats and dogs. Keep the essentials visible and make ordinary care easier to share.

A useful place to begin

Good company is a shared household notebook for the ordinary routines of living with cats and dogs. It helps keep care tasks and food inventory visible.

Using this notebook

This is not veterinary advice. The pantry tool calculates stock from a daily amount you already use; it does not recommend portions or diets. Ask a veterinarian about health and nutrition concerns.

Your privacy

Tools run in this browser. Entries are not uploaded, and the pages load no remote fonts, analytics or advertising. Saved choices and checklist states use this browser’s local storage, scoped to this notebook. Clear site data in your browser to remove them. If storage is unavailable, tools still work and choices last only until the page closes.

Downloads are files created in your browser, not files sent to a service. The hosting server may keep ordinary access logs according to its owner’s settings. No accounts, orders or bookings are accepted here.

Make it your own

The illustrations and learning examples were created for this notebook. Its content is intended to be read, tried and adapted. Begin with the reference pages or open the local tool.
