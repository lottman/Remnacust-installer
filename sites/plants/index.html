Read the light, not the room.

Bright, indirect

A luminous spot with no long spell of harsh direct sun. Observe it over a whole day.

Gentle daylight

Near a window but away from the strongest rays. Seasonal light changes matter.

Lower light

Growth slows down. Most plants still need daylight; no plant lives indefinitely in darkness.
