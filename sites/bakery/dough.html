Scale a simple dough.

Baker’s percentages use flour weight as 100%. This calculator estimates ingredients only; fermentation and baking instructions must come from your recipe.

Flour is the reference.

For 500 g of flour, 68% hydration means 340 g of water. Wholegrain flours often need more water and more resting time.

This basic calculation excludes starter, seeds and other additions. If your recipe contains a starter, count its flour and water separately.
